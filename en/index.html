About

The partner you meet is the partner who does the work.

BeON was founded by two patent attorneys who decided to keep the work in their own hands. Between us we cover electrical and information engineering, AI and machine learning and semiconductors on one side, and molecular biology, pharmaceuticals and cosmetics on the other — a technical range wide enough that most matters stay in-house from the first call to registration. Nothing is passed down a chain: the attorney who takes your call drafts your specification and argues your case.

Practice

Every stage of turning technology into a right.

From pre-filing strategy to post-registration disputes, we handle the full lifecycle of an IP right.

i.

Incoming — Korean Filings for Foreign Applicants

We act as Korean counsel for overseas applicants and their foreign associates: Paris Convention filings, PCT national phase entry, prosecution before KIPO, and Korean trademark and design applications. One partner completed his undergraduate and graduate studies in Japan and works directly from Japanese-language originals, removing a translation layer on Japan-originating matters.

KIPO · PCT national phase · Japanese capability · Foreign associate support
ii.

Korean Patents

Prior art searching and claim architecture come first; the specification follows. We draft for scope that survives examination, and answer office actions with substantive argument rather than reflexive narrowing.

Drafting · Office actions · Registration
iii.

International Filings

We design PCT and direct-filing strategies, select entry jurisdictions against your commercial map, and coordinate with local counsel in the United States, Europe, Japan and China.

PCT · US · EP · JP · CN
iv.

Trademarks & Designs

Protection for brand names and product appearance — clearance searching, filing, opposition and portfolio management as the business grows.

Trademarks · Designs · Brand portfolio
v.

Trials & Litigation

Appeals against decisions of refusal, invalidation and scope-confirmation trials, infringement warnings and responses. When a right is contested, we defend it.

Appeals · Invalidation · Infringement
vi.

Government Programs & Patent Analytics

Support for government-funded IP and R&D programs, patent landscape analysis, and advisory work on portfolio and technology strategy.

IP R&D · Landscape analysis · Advisory
Process

Complex matters, one step at a time.

A founding partner makes the call at each stage, and you see where the matter stands throughout. Questions are welcome at any point.

01

Consultation

We work out what the invention actually is, and what the business needs from it.

02

Prior Art Search

We assess registrability and realistic scope, then fix the strategy.

03

Drafting

The invention becomes a specification — broad where it can be, defensible where it must be.

04

Filing

Korean and foreign filings, with schedules and costs managed on your behalf.

05

Office Action Response

We analyse the examiner’s grounds and answer them directly through amendment and argument.

06

Registration & Maintenance

Registration is completed, and annuities and portfolio are managed from there.
